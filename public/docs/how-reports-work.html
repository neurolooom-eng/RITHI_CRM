How the reports work

What each report counts, where every value comes from, and who may take the file. This page covers the seven screens under Reports: Consumption Report, KPI Export, Not Consumed Against this Call, Call Report, Customer Feedback Report, Feedback Without a Report and Hand Stock Report — what one row of each is, which date its filter reads, the columns it carries, what the download looks like, which permission opens it and whose records it shows. Read the middle column for what happens, the left for what the report reads, and the right for what the system refuses and what it demands.

Flow 1

Pick the rows, pick the columns, take the file — Consumption, Call and Customer Feedback Reports

Five reports share one page. The first five entries under Reports open one screen, Reports, with a tab per report; the menu entry and the tab both lead to the same place, and the tab strip shows only the reports your role may open. The three in this flow are built the same way: a filter, a column picker, and two buttons.

The filter runs in the database, not on what has loaded. The number beside the buttons is the database's count of every matching row, so it is exact and carries no +. The download then reads every matching row, a thousand at a time, before the file is written.

Every row is one you may see. Each report reads the registers as you, under the same row rules as the register screens. A short or empty report from a role that sees only its own and its team's records is a statement about that role, not about the register.

Reads

  • Your role's permissions — which report keys it holds

Flow 1 · Step 01

Reports — a tab per report

Reports (all of them)↳ Consumption Report↳ KPI Export↳ Not Consumed Against this Call↳ Call Report↳ Customer Feedback Report

Each of the five has its own box on Roles & Permissions under Reports. Reports (all of them) grants all five: a role holding it sees every tab, and a role given one report sees only that tab and that menu entry.

Feedback Without a Report and Hand Stock Report sit in the same menu group but are pages of their own, each with its own box — Reports (all of them) does not grant either (Flow 3).

Restrictions & mandates

  • Without a report's key, its address shows "🔒 You don’t have access to this module. Ask an administrator to grant it in Roles & Permissions."
  • Without a database connection: "Not connected to the database — a report needs it."
  • PolicyThe page keys are checked by the application. The rows are decided by the row rules of the registers each report reads — the key adds none.

Reads

  • The spare consumption register — one row per spare line booked
  • The call each line names, by UCN
  • The call's latest-entered visit

Flow 1 · Step 02

Consumption Report — one row per spare booked

↳ Consumption ReportView consumptionView callsView reports

The tab is headed Spare Consumption: "One row per spare booked, with its call and that call’s latest visit around it." A line that was voided stays in the report with QTY 0.

Filter: Call date from / to (the call's registration date, both days included), Product, Customer, City, Engineer, Part (code or name), UCN — each matching any part of the value — and Call type: Field, Installation and PM pick calls whose type contains FIELD, INSTALL and P M. Engineer matches the Visiting Service Engineer; Part matches the code or the description. Clear the filter empties every box, and the line beside it states the filter in words.

The two visit dates come from the visit, not from the spare line. They are the call's latest entry — the visit most recently written to the register, the same one the call's status comes from — not the latest visit date. Where the call has no visit they fall back to what the import file said, and then to when a spare was first booked on that call; read those as "no later than", not "on". Visit UID is blank on exactly those rows.

Restrictions & mandates

  • Without any of View consumption, View calls or View reports, the buttons are greyed: "You can open this page but not take the file — that needs the right to read consumption, calls or visit reports. Ask an administrator under Roles & Permissions."
  • PolicyLines are those your role may see in Spare Consumption: an office role or View all data sees all; anyone else sees lines they booked, lines in their own email, and lines in their own or their team's name.
  • PolicyThe call and visit columns fill only where you may also see the call and its visit. A line on a call you cannot see comes out with its call columns blank — and so drops out of any call-date filter (tested).

Reads

  • The calls — all three registers, cancelled calls included
  • The call's visits, counted, and the latest-entered one
  • The spare lines booked on the call, voided ones left out

Flow 1 · Step 03

Call Report — one row per call

↳ Call ReportView callsView reports

"One row per CALL — not per visit, so a call attended four times appears once." Field, Installation and PM calls are all here, and so are cancelled ones.

Filter: Call date from / to (the registration date, both days included), Product, Customer, City, Allotted to, UCN — each matching any part — then Call type and Call status, which must match exactly.

Call Status is worked out, not typed. It reads Unattended (no visit), Unsolved, Report pending, Solved or Cancelled, from the latest-entered visit. Status (as keyed) and Last Visit Status are what somebody typed, and can disagree with it. Last Visit columns are the latest entry, not the latest visit date — a report written up late does not outrank a visit made after it. Spare Lines, Spare Qty and Spares Used leave voided lines (QTY 0) out.

Restrictions & mandates

  • Without View calls or View reports the buttons are greyed: "…that needs the right to read calls or visit reports."
  • PolicyThe calls are exactly those you see in the call registers: View calls, and then every call for an office role or View all data, otherwise calls registered by you or filed to your desk, calls allotted to you or your team, and calls allotted to nobody. Without View calls the report is empty.
  • PolicyVisits, Spare Lines and Spare Qty count only the visits and lines you may see.
  • The Call status picker offers the five values the column holds: Unattended, Unsolved, Report pending, Solved and Cancelled.

Reads

  • The customer feedback register — one feedback per call

Flow 1 · Step 04

Customer Feedback Report — one row per feedback

↳ Customer Feedback ReportView feedbackRecord customer feedback on a visit (any register)

"One row per FEEDBACK — one per call, since a second feedback for a call replaces the first." Each question is its own column, named after the export's own heading.

Filter: Feedback date from / to — the feedback's own date, never Loaded On: for a migrated row the date the export gave it, for one recorded here the moment it was recorded — then Product, Customer, State, Engineer, UCN (any part), Call type, and Source: Uploaded (it came from the v2Feedback export) or Entered here.

A blank on a question is not a missing answer. Operating Feasibility and General Support are asked of every visit; the four PM / field questions of a PM or field visit; the installation questions of an installation. A blank means the question was not put. All Answers carries every answer as given, including any question no column has been made for.

Restrictions & mandates

  • Without View feedback or Record customer feedback on a visit the buttons are greyed: "…that needs the right to read customer feedback."
  • PolicyEither key reads every feedback in the register, whoever the call belongs to — feedback is not scoped by call or team (tested; see Not yet in place).

Reads

  • Every matching row, read a thousand at a time
  • The columns you ticked

Flow 1 · Step 05

Columns, then ⭳ Download Excel or ⭳ CSV

Export / download CSV

The locked columns are the report's own format, shown ticked and greyed — "a rule rather than an omission". The rest are ticked one by one; Add every column, Just the report format and, on the Consumption Report, Back to the default columns. The Consumption Report starts with Line ID, Source Ref Key and Created At ticked; they can be unticked. The file's columns are always in the report's own order, not the order they were ticked.

⭳ Download Excel writes consumption-, call-report- or feedback-report- followed by today's date as year-month-day, .xlsx, with two sheets. The first holds the rows: dates are real Excel dates shown dd-mmm-yyyy hh:mm:ss (dd-mmm-yyyy where no time was recorded), in your own time rather than the database's, and numbers stay numbers. The second, Filter, records the report, what one row is, the filter in words, the row count, the columns (N mandatory + N chosen), when it was downloaded, and the report's own notes. ⭳ CSV writes the same rows as text, dates spelled dd-MMM-yyyy HH:mm:ss (dd-MMM-yyyy where no time was recorded), with no second sheet.

The result reads "Downloaded N rows, N columns." and the download is recorded in the Audit Log.

Restrictions & mandates

  • Both buttons are greyed while the count is 0 ("Nothing matches that filter.") and while a download is running ("Building… N rows").
  • A failed count says "Could not count the matches — the download will still read them."
  • MandateThe CSV needs Export / download CSV: "Exporting / downloading data is not permitted for your role." The Excel file does not ask for it — see Not yet in place.
  • Extra (import) and All Answers hold several values each, and come out as their JSON text.

Flow 2

Fixed-format exports — KPI Export, Not Consumed Against this Call

No column picker. Each of these has one shape. The count on the button is the database's count for the range chosen, so it is exact; the download reads every page before the file is written.

Reads

  • Field and Installation calls — not PM, and not cancelled
  • Each call's visits and spare requests

Flow 2 · Step 01

KPI Export — the workbook's Field_INST tab

↳ KPI Export

One row per call: the KPI workbook's columns A to AG, in its order and under its own headings (spelling included — Call Registeration Date), plus Pending Days, which the workbook does not have. Field and Installation calls only; cancelled calls are not included at all.

Registered from / to read the call's registration date and time; the to day is included to 23:59:59 India time. Whole register clears both. The button reads ⭳ Excel — N calls.

⭳ Excel writes kpi-field-inst-<from or start>_<to or today>.xlsx (or the day's date when no range is set) with one sheet, Field_INST: dates are real Excel dates, numbers stay numbers. ⭳ CSV writes dates as dd-MMM-yyyy, and the registration date with its time — which Excel may not read as a date. The result reads "Exported N calls."

Restrictions & mandates

  • The page key is the whole right: no further key is asked before the Excel file.
  • PolicyThe calls are those you see in the Field Call and Installation registers (needs View calls); the visit columns read only visits you may see.
  • The CSV needs Export / download CSV.
  • The screen says Report Pending counts as Open; the file says Close — see Not yet in place.

Flow 2 · Step 02

Not Consumed Against this Call — parts sent and not booked

↳ Not Consumed Against this CallView consumptionView callsView reports

One row per call and part. A part sent on two orders for one call is added together, and compared with everything booked of that part code on that call. Finding reads Not used where none was booked and Short where less was booked than sent (2 sent, 1 used). Parts are matched on the part code, not the description.

Only calls whose status reads Solved are listed — not Report pending, not open calls, where the part may still be in the van. Dispatched counts as reached: acknowledging a delivery is not mandatory. A line refused by an approver or dropped by Stores is not here — nothing arrived. Stage says Received where any line was acknowledged, otherwise Dispatched.

Filter: Dispatched from / to (the date the part was sent, not the call date), Engineer (a list of the engineers this report holds), Product, Part code. Preview shows the first 25 rows with "First 25 of N". ⭳ Download Excel (N) writes not-consumed-against-call-<year-month-day>.xlsx — sheets Not Consumed and About (what it lists and excludes, the filter, rows, the day taken); ⭳ CSV the same rows as text. The file's first two headings are spelled ucn and call_number.

Restrictions & mandates

  • The download buttons are greyed without View consumption, View calls or View reports, and while the count is 0; this report shows no note saying why.
  • PolicyRows need a call you may see (and so View calls) and a spare request you may see.
  • The CSV needs Export / download CSV.
  • Nothing flagged: "Nothing is flagged for that filter — nothing to download."

Flow 3

Checks that load in full — Feedback Without a Report, Hand Stock Report

Pages of their own, outside Reports (all of them). Each has its own box on Roles & Permissions under Reports, and was given to administrators to begin with; any other role is a tick. Both load every row onto the screen, then search and download what is shown.

Reads

  • Every customer feedback
  • The call its UCN names, and every visit on it

Flow 3 · Step 01

Feedback Without a Report — the visit happened, the write-up did not

Open: Feedback Without a Report

One row per feedback that has no Solved - Report Completed visit behind it. Missing names which of four: the feedback records no UCN · no call with that UCN · no visit at all · a visit exists but none reads Solved - Report Completed. For the last, Latest visit status says what the latest-entered visit reads instead.

Any completed visit on the call clears it, not only the latest. The status is matched on its letters and digits only, so a trailing space, lower case or an en-dash still counts as completed. Chips filter by finding and add up to the row count; search covers UCN, call number, party, product, serial and engineer. ⭳ Excel writes feedback-without-a-report-<year-month-day>.xlsx with an About sheet (each finding explained, the chip and search applied, rows, the day taken); ⭳ CSV the same 21 columns as text.

Restrictions & mandates

  • Given by migration to Admin, Technical Support and Zoho Migration.
  • PolicyThe feedback, the call and the visits are each read under their own row rules. A role that cannot see the call is told no call with that UCN for a call that exists (tested) — see Not yet in place.
  • An empty list says "Every customer feedback has a completed service report behind it." only to a reader who sees every record; anyone else is told "No customer feedback that you can see is missing a completed service report."
  • The CSV needs Export / download CSV.

Reads

  • The hand stock balances — the same figures the Hand Stock register shows, derived from every movement

Flow 3 · Step 02

Hand Stock Report — every line, then the file

Open: Hand Stock Report

One line per engineer and part. It loads a thousand lines at a time until every line is in; the count carries a + and reads "N loaded…" while pages are arriving, then "N lines, complete". Nothing is stored: the figures are worked out from the movements, so the report and the Hand Stock register cannot disagree.

The screen shows Engineer, Part Code, Part, Opening, Stock Out, Consumed, Trf In, Trf Out, Returned, On Hand and Last Movement; a negative On Hand is shown inverted — more was consumed than this system knows was issued. Search covers engineer, email, part code and part.

Three formats, named HandStock_dd-MMM-yyyy_HHmmss: ⭳ CSV (text; a searched file adds _SEARCH-<term> to its name), ⭳ Excel (.xlsx) (numbers stay numbers, dates stay dates) and ⭳ Excel (.xls), the Excel 2003 format, which Excel may warn about before opening. Both workbooks carry an About sheet: what it is, that every page was loaded, the scope (every engineer, or your own and your team's, and any search), rows, the day taken.

Restrictions & mandates

  • MandateAll three buttons stay greyed until every line has loaded: "Still loading every line — the download opens when the whole report is in."
  • Given by migration to Admin and Technical Support; the menu entry asks for the same key as the page.
  • PolicyAn office role or View all data sees every engineer; anyone else sees their own stock and their team's. The subtitle says which.
  • The CSV needs Export / download CSV.
  • Other ± is On Hand less the named columns — the stock adjustments — so every row adds up.

Why two reports give different counts for the same period

Each report counts a different thing, dates it by a different date, and leaves different records out. Two totals that disagree are usually both right. Before acting on a difference, check the three columns on the right.

The Call Report against Visit Reports differs in both directions at once: a call with several visits is one row there and several here; a call with no visit yet is one row there and none here. The Consumption Report against the Call Report's Spare Lines: the first counts voided lines, the second leaves them out. And the same person can be shown different numbers by two reports with nothing missing, because each reads its registers under that register's own row rules — calls by call visibility, consumption by whose lines they are, feedback not scoped at all.

The records kept

  • Audit Log. Every download from these screens is recorded: report.consumption, report.call-report and report.feedback-report with rows, columns and the filter; kpi.export with the range and format; report.unused_spares with rows, filter and format; report.feedback_without_report and report.handstock with rows, scope and format.
  • The file itself. Every workbook except the KPI Export carries a second sheet stating what it is, the filter or scope, the rows and when it was taken.
  • Nothing else. The reports change no record; they read.

Not yet in place

Stated here so nobody reads this page as describing them as done.

  • Excel downloads skip Export / download CSV (D-018, D-065). A role refused the CSV takes the same rows as .xlsx or .xls. The KPI Export asks no report right beyond its page key. And on the Consumption, Call and Customer Feedback Reports, a refused CSV is still recorded in the Audit Log and reported as "Downloaded N rows" (D-065).
  • Customer feedback is not scoped (D-037). View feedback, or Record customer feedback on a visit, reads every feedback row; the Customer Feedback Report shows a feedback on a call the reader cannot see (tested).
  • KPI Export: the screen and the file disagree on Report Pending (D-095). The screen says Close only for Solved - Report Completed and Open for Report Pending; the file writes Close for Report Pending and leaves its Pending Days blank (tested).
  • Feedback Without a Report is only right for a reader who sees every call (D-096). A role granted it that cannot see a call, or its completed visit, is shown that feedback as missing — no call with that UCN for a call that exists (tested).
  • Consumption Report for a reader who cannot see the call (D-096): the line is listed with its call columns blank and its visit dates falling back to the booking time, although the call has a visit (tested).
