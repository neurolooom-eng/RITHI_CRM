RITHI CRM · Master · process reference

How the masters work

What every form picks from. The customer, the machine, the product line, the part, the person and the short value lists behind the dropdowns are each kept in one master, and the rest of the application reads them. This page covers the seven screens under Master: Party Master, Product Database, Product Database 2.0, Product Master, User Master, Part Master and All Masters — what each is for, what you can do there, what it refuses, which permission each action needs, and where its values are used. Read the middle column for what happens, the left for what the step reads, and the right for what the system refuses and what it demands.

Flow 1

The customer — Party Master

One row per customer. The party name is the key: machines, calls and contracts name the customer by that text, so the name is matched ignoring capitals and spaces at either end, and it is not editable on this screen. Each party is also given a short Key — Party-1, Party-2 … — on the day it is first added, and keeps it for ever.

Three rights, not one (since 30-Sep-2026): editing a party's record, verifying its KYC, and changing the Serviceman on every party at once are ticked separately on Roles & Permissions. Edit masters (all of the below) is the parent of all three — a role holding it has every one.

Reads

  • The Party Master — every signed-in user may read it
  • A copy kept on this device, shown at once and refreshed every 30 minutes

Flow 1 · Step 01

Find a customer

Open: Party Master

The list opens from the copy on this device and says when it was synced; ↻ Refresh reads it again. Typing in Party name, State, City or Type searches the database directly. Parties load 1,000 at a time with Load more, and while more remain the count carries a +.

The table opens with Key, Party Name, City, State, Type, Profile, Address, Pincode, Serviceman, Phone, Email, KYC, KYC Records, GSTIN and PAN; ⚙ Columns offers every other field on the row. ⭳ Export CSV downloads the columns shown.

Clicking a party opens it for editing if you may edit it, and otherwise opens a read-only view that says "Read only. Changing a party needs the right to edit masters." There is no Add button on this screen: parties are added by the Party Master upload (Step 05).

Restrictions & mandates

  • PolicyReading is open to every signed-in user — the call forms pick customers from it. The page itself opens only with Open: Party Master.

Reads

  • The party's row, re-read after saving

Flow 1 · Step 02

Edit a party

Add / edit master records (parties, parts, products, lists)

The drawer has three groups. The customer: Type, Profile, Serviceman, Route. Where the machine is — the installation address, "what a call sends somebody to": Address, City, State, Pincode, Phone, Phone 2, Fax, Email. Where the bill goes — "Left blank means the same as above": Address, Pincode, Phone, Phone 2, Fax, Email. Then the KYC block (Step 03). Save writes them and re-reads the row, so what the database filled in is what you see.

The party name is shown in the title and not offered for editing: "Every machine, call and contract names this customer by it, so changing it would strand them — ask for a rename rather than typing over it."

Restrictions & mandates

  • PolicyChanging a party needs Add / edit master records (or its parent, Edit masters).
  • TriggerThe Key cannot be changed once given; the party name is trimmed of spaces at either end on every save.
  • TriggerThe party name cannot be changed through the data interface either (0335): "A party's name is the key every machine, call and contract names it by, so it is not changed here". A change of capital letters or outer spaces is allowed; a re-load by Bulk Uploads matches on the name and does not change it. This is also what keeps the delete guard honest — a party cannot be renamed first and then deleted.

Reads

  • The Tax columns the party arrived with, for GSTIN and PAN
  • Your sign-in, to stamp who verified

Flow 1 · Step 03

KYC — status, numbers and records

Verify a party’s KYCAdd / edit master records

Every customer starts Pending; the other statuses are Verified and Rejected. Marking one Verified records who did it and when, from the sign-in; moving it off Verified clears both again. The register shows ✓ KYC Verified on the row, and KYC Records links to each attached document.

GSTIN and PAN. Where either is blank, the database fills it from the Tax columns the party was loaded with; a GSTIN contains a PAN, so a GSTIN alone gives both. A number already in the box is never replaced by this.

⤴ Attach a KYC record uploads a file to Drive and saves the link at once, with who attached it and when; Remove unlinks it and leaves the file in Drive. Verified with nothing attached still stands — the drawer says "Marked Verified with no record attached. The status stands — attaching the certificate is what lets somebody else check it."

The KYC of each customer is also shown beside the request on Request Registration, with a filter on it.

Restrictions & mandates

  • TriggerChanging the status without the KYC right: "RBAC: changing a party's KYC status needs "Verify KYC"". Without it the Status box is greyed and says "Changing the status needs the “Verify a party’s KYC” permission."
  • CheckThe status can only be Pending, Verified or Rejected.
  • A KYC record larger than 10 MB is refused before upload.
  • Attaching and removing records is offered only to holders of Add / edit master records.

Reads

  • Every Serviceman spelling on the Party Master, with how many customers carry it
  • The User Master names

Flow 1 · Step 04

✎ Change engineer — one Serviceman, every customer

Swap the Serviceman on every party at once

The Serviceman on a party is who looks after that customer. On a new call it fills Call Allocated To where the machine has no Service Engineer of its own — the machine's engineer wins where it has one. It is a suggestion, not an assignment: whoever registers the call can change it.

Because a call is allotted by name, a Serviceman spelled differently from the User Master names somebody who does not exist. Change this lists every spelling with its count, marking those "⚠ not in User Master"; To this offers only User Master names, or Leave nobody — this engineer has gone. Before you press it, the drawer says how many customers will change, and that "Calls already registered keep the engineer they were allotted to."

Restrictions & mandates

  • FunctionWithout the right: "RBAC: changing the Serviceman on every party needs "Swap the Serviceman in bulk"".
  • Mandate"Pick the name to change"; and the two names must differ — "That is the same name".
  • The match is on the exact stored spelling, so a second spelling is never caught by accident.
  • The new name cannot be typed — it must come from the User Master.

Reads

  • The Party Master file, loaded under Bulk Uploads → Masters

Flow 2

Machines and product lines

Three screens with similar names that hold different things. Product Database is the install base — one row per machine, its customer and its cover. Product Database 2.0 is the same machines worked out from the cover registers instead of stored, kept beside the first so the two can be compared. Product Master is the catalogue — one row per product code, not per machine.

A machine is its model and its serial, never the serial alone: serial numbers repeat across models, so both registers of machines key on the two together.

Reads

  • The install base, with Status, Contract, INST Call and Engineer worked out when read (Step 02)
  • A copy of the machines and customers on this device, and the line above the table says how old it is

Flow 2 · Step 02 · automatic

How a machine gets there, and what is worked out

Stored — by product AND serial, never the serial alone. The Product Database upload adds or corrects machines. A Warranty Register sale adds each machine, or updates it — warranty number and dates, PM visits, invoice number and date, warranty years and months, accessories included. A Contract Register entry gives that machine its contract number, dates, type, status and PM visits (which replace the sale's), and adds a machine not yet held. An Ownership Transfer moves that one machine to its new owner, with the new owner's Party Master address and the transfer's Ref and Date. The owner written is the machine's current owner: whichever is later of its latest sale entry and its latest ownership transfer.

Where the warranty starts. The installing engineer chooses on the installation report — Installation Call Solved Date or Invoice Date. The first starts the machine's warranty on the day the installation call was solved and ends it one warranty period later; the second keeps the sale's start. Each installation's choice, solved date and resulting warranty are kept in the installation warranty record, one row per call, which Bulk Uploads can fill for installations back to 2018.

Worked out each time it is read. Status: WGP while the warranty end date is today or later; otherwise, while a contract that names this machine and its current customer runs to today or later, AMC for a labour contract, CMC for a comprehensive one, or CONTRACT (TYPE NOT RECORDED) where the contract has no type; otherwise OGP. The Contract and INST Call shown are the ones naming this machine and its current customer, so a machine re-sold stops showing the previous owner's. The Engineer is the customer's Serviceman on the Party Master, and is blank where the Party Master does not carry the customer.

Warranty Status and Contract Status among the 32 columns are the words the file carried, not the status worked out from the dates; the two can disagree.

Restrictions & mandates

  • MandateThe upload needs Item Serial Number and Item Name on every row; a row missing either is held back.
  • In this upload a blank cell clears its column: the file is the machine's whole row, so a blank means "none".
  • PolicyWriting machines through the upload needs Add / edit master records; the screen itself offers no edit.

Reads

  • Warranty Register — the sale's machine lines
  • Contract Register — the contract's machine lines
  • Additional Entry Details — machines recovered by hand
  • Ownership Transfer
  • The installation call — its Warranty Start Date or solved date

Flow 2 · Step 03

Product Database 2.0 — the machine as the registers describe it

Open: Product Database 2.0Rebuild Product Database 2.0

One row per machine that the warranty sale, contract or additional entry register names with both a model and a serial. The model and serial are compared with spaces, punctuation and capitals ignored. Every answer says where it came from: Party from, Because, and the warranty and contract sources.

Whose machine. The latest dated record wins — an ownership transfer, an additional entry, a contract or the warranty sale — with a tie going to the ownership transfer. Warranty start is the installation call's Warranty Start Date, or failing that the date it was solved, then the additional entry, then the sale. Status is worked out on every read, warranty first: WGP, then AMC / CMC, then OGP, with a contract of no recognised type reading CONTRACT (TYPE NOT RECORDED).

Search by product, serial, party, contract or SA number, and filter with the status chips (exact counts). Clicking a row opens the whole record, with links to the sale, contract, transfer, installation call and Machine History. ⭳ Export CSV gives every column.

The machines are assembled ahead of time. The header says "Live as of …", or "A register has changed since … — updating within 5 minutes"; a change to any of the registers marks it for a rebuild, which runs on a five-minute schedule. ⟳ Rebuild from the registers does it now, without blocking anybody reading.

Restrictions & mandates

  • FunctionRebuilding without the right: "Your role may not rebuild Product Database 2.0." The button is shown only to holders of Rebuild Product Database 2.0.
  • Every signed-in user who can open the screen reads every machine, with its warranty and contract detail.
  • A register row with a serial and no model, or a model and no serial, is left out rather than guessed.
  • An empty screen says why: for each register, the rows held and how many have no serial or no model.

Reads

  • The Product Master file (Bulk Uploads → Product Master (product lines))

Flow 2 · Step 04

Product Master — the catalogue of product lines

Open: Product Master (product lines)

One row per Product Code: Product Name, Type, Category, Short Form, Still sold?, Added and Added by. Several codes can share a name. Search by code, name, type, category or short form; the chips All, Active and Inactive carry exact counts. ⭳ Export CSV.

Lines are loaded and corrected by the upload, matched on the Product Code; a line the file does not clearly mark Inactive comes in Active. ＋ Add entry, ✎ Edit and 🗑 Delete are on the screen, each with its own permission; Edit changes everything but the Product Code.

Inactive stops the line being offered for a new sale on the Warranty Register — the Product Code and Product Name pickers on a sale's machine list Active lines only, and the sale says how many lines are retired. Contracts, calls, visits, spares and feedback on machines already sold carry on.

Restrictions & mandates

  • MandateThe upload needs Product Code and Product Name on every row.
  • PolicyWriting lines needs the Product Master's add, edit or delete permission (or Add / edit master records); reading is open to every signed-in user.
  • TriggerThe Product Code is never changed (0335): "A product line's code is the key machines name it by, so it is not changed" — not by the screen and not through the data interface.

Flow 3

Parts — Part Master

The item catalogue. A part is listed as CODE|Description, and that string is its identity: spare requests, dispatches, consumption, hand stock, transfers and returns all name the part by it. So the code and description are changed only by a rename, which carries every one of those records with it.

Reads

  • The whole catalogue, on opening and every 30 minutes
  • Product Database product names, for the Product filter

Flow 3 · Step 01

Find a part

Open: Part Master

Columns: Part Code, Description, Item Detail, Spare / Consumable, Product, HSN Code, Purchase Cost, Active. 🔍 Search everything matches every word you type anywhere in a part, in any order; there are boxes for Part code and Description, a product filter (a product, Common (all products) or ⚠ Unrecognised product) and Active / Inactive. A product the Product Database does not name shows in red with ⚠. ⭳ Export CSV.

Restrictions & mandates

  • PolicyReading is open to every signed-in user.
  • Without Add / edit master records the screen has no Add, Edit, Deactivate or tick boxes.

Reads

  • Product Database product names

Flow 3 · Step 03

✎ Edit, and rename

Add / edit master recordsRename a part (moves every record that names it)

Spare / Consumable, Product, Purchase cost and HSN Code are ordinary fields. A Spare / Consumable value the Item Master brought that is not one of the four is kept and offered at the top. All of it moves in one go, or none of it, so hand stock is the same afterwards. The button reads Rename and save.

  • Function"Another part is already called "…" — a rename cannot merge two parts"
  • Function"A part needs both a code and a description" · "Neither the code nor the description may contain "|""

People and value lists

The User Master holds everybody, signed in or not, and the reporting line: a manager's team is worked out from the Reporting and Regional Manager each row names. A call is allotted to a User Master name, which is why the name is required. Designation is the job somebody holds; Role is what the application lets them do, and the two often differ.

The records kept

  • Party Key. Party-1, Party-2 … given once, on first insert, and never changed.
  • KYC. Who verified and when, from the sign-in, cleared again if the status leaves Verified; each attached record keeps its file name, link, who attached it and when.
  • The migrated system's answers. The Product Database keeps the file's own Item Status, Service Engineer, contract number and INST Call beside the values it works out, so the two can be compared.
  • Part renames. Every record naming the part moves with it in one go, or none does.
  • Product Database 2.0 records when it was last built and whether a register has changed since.
  • System columns. Every table also records which login created and last changed each row, and when, written by the database.

Not yet in place

Stated here so nobody reads this page as describing them as done.

  • Editing an existing person needs an administrator (D-084). The User Master offers Edit, the Department bar and the role to holders of Edit User Master details, but the database refuses any change to an existing row's name, email, designation, managers, region, Active or role from anybody who is not an administrator — and a change to Department, phone or address only from somebody holding Dispatch / DC (Stores). Adding a new person is not affected.
  • Verify a party’s KYC, held alone, changes nothing (D-085). The Party Master opens read-only without Add / edit master records, and the database writes no party change without it.
  • A value list's Add is hidden from a holder of Add / edit master records alone (D-086). The database accepts that key for every list; the screen asks for the list's own key or Edit masters.
  • KYC is shown, not enforced. Nothing refuses a sale or an installation call for a customer whose KYC is not Verified.
  • An Inactive product line can still be typed on a new sale. The picker does not offer it, but the box accepts a typed name.
  • Spare Approval Reason is read by no screen — the Commercial and NSM approval reasons are a fixed list on the form.
