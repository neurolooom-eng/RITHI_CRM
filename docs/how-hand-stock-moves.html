RITHI CRM · Hand stock · process reference

How hand stock moves

Hand stock is derived, never stored. There is no balance column anybody can edit — what an engineer holds is computed, every time it is read, from the movements that put it there and took it away. So this is one document rather than five: consumption, reconciliation, returns, transfers and adjustments are not separate systems, they are entries in one ledger, and the only way to understand any of them is to see what they do to the same number. How a spare is asked for, approved and booked out by Stores is the companion document, How a spare moves.

Which makes consumption the control point. Every consumption line, however it is written, is capped at the balance by the database — a hard refusal, not a warning — and so are a transfer, a return and an adjustment that removes stock. A period close can carry everything up to a date into one opening figure per engineer and part, after which only later movements are read; no screen offers it.

In · the ordinary way

Stock Out — what a booking does to the balance

The approved spare leaves Stores and lands in an engineer's hand stock. How it gets that far — the approvals, Pending Dispatch, the Delivery Challan and the receipt — is in How a spare moves. This is what the booking does to the number.

Reads

  • The stock out and the quantity each line sent
  • The engineer on the request — whose balance it lands in

Stock Out · into the balance

Counted from the moment it is booked

Dispatch / DC (Stores)

The parts count in the engineer's hand stock from the time the stock out is booked — not from the DC date typed on the challan, and not from the acknowledgement. A line sent short adds what was sent; the rest adds when it goes.

A part issued as ♻ Refurbished lands under the refurbished part's own code.

Restrictions & mandates

  • The acknowledgement changes nothing here. Received is a confirmation on the request; the balance moved at booking.
  • A dropped spare adds nothing: it was never booked out.
  • Once any spare on an order has gone out, the engineer on that order cannot be changed — the stock is moved with a Stock Transfer instead.

Reads

  • The opening file — engineer, part, quantity, the date the pool was struck and its Source

Out · 1 of 3

Consumption — the part is fitted

Stock leaves the engineer's hands and goes into a machine. It is recorded two ways: the engineer writes it on the visit, or the office books it afterwards as a reconciliation. Both reduce the balance identically. What differs is who wrote it and what it had to justify.

Reads

  • The call and its visit — the spare is booked on the visit it was used on
  • Hand Stock — this engineer's balance; the picker offers only what they hold

Consumption · the ordinary way

On the visit report

Book spares used on a visit (any register)

In Call Reporting, Add Consumption? opens a picker of the engineer's hand stock — the call's product, its accessories and the common parts, or everything in hand. The visit is saved first and the spares after it. The line carries the engineer, the part, the quantity and the call, and is marked Report.

Restrictions & mandates

  • TriggerYou cannot consume more than the engineer holds: "… has 2 of … in hand, so 3 cannot be consumed. Ask the Spare Coordinator to correct the hand stock first." The fix is the coordinator's, not the engineer's.
  • TriggerA visit on the call: "No visit has been filed on … yet, so a spare cannot be booked against it. File the visit report first — the spare is recorded on the visit it was used on."
  • TriggerA quantity above zero: "Quantity must be more than zero".
  • PolicyNeeds Book spares used on a visit (any register) (or Dispatch / DC (Stores)); the form says so before the visit is saved.
  • A line loaded by a bulk upload, carrying its source reference, is a record of what was used and is not capped.

Reads

  • The call register — the UCN must exist; it fills the call number and engineer
  • Hand Stock — of the engineer named, not of whoever is typing

Consumption · the corrected way

Reconciliation — the office books it

Add consumption against a call (reconciliation)

On Spare Consumption, ＋ Add consumption (or the Reco action on a call): enter the UCN and tab out to pull the call in, confirm the engineer whose hand stock it comes off, pick the parts from that stock with a quantity and an optional GRIR / traceability reference each, and give the reason. The line is marked Reconciliation rather than Report.

No visit is needed. A reconciliation exists for a part fitted and never reported, so the missing visit is the reason for it.

Restrictions & mandates

  • TriggerEach refused by name: "A reconciliation needs the UCN of the call the spare was used on" · "No call found with UCN … — check the number" · "A reconciliation needs the engineer whose hand stock the spare came off" · "A reconciliation needs the part" · "A reconciliation needs a reason (why the spare is being booked by hand)".
  • TriggerCapped at the balance, the same as any other consumption: "… has 2 of … in hand — cannot book 3 against this call".
  • PolicyA line marked Reconciliation needs Add consumption against a call (reconciliation), whatever screen or route writes it.

Reads

  • The line as it stands, and the engineer's balance of that part

Consumption · correcting one

A wrong line is VOIDED, never deleted

Add consumption against a call (reconciliation)

On Spare Consumption, ✎ on the line: give the new quantity and Why. Set it to 0 to void the line — it stays on record, reads Voided, and the spare goes back to the engineer's stock. Only the quantity can change.

Consumption lines cannot be deleted from the application, and the database blocks it outright. The adjustment reason and the name of who adjusted it stay on the line, and the database's audit trail keeps the line as it was before and after. The line itself also keeps its original quantity (before the first change) and when it was last adjusted.

Restrictions & mandates

  • TriggerA reason, in these words: "Say why the line is being voided — the reason is kept with it" · "Say why the quantity is being adjusted — the reason is kept with the line".
  • Trigger"Quantity cannot be negative" — the form adds "use 0 to void the line". Raising a line is capped at the balance: "Only … left in …'s hand stock for …".
  • Trigger"A reconciliation can only change the quantity — not the call, part, engineer or source".
  • TriggerA delete: "RECORD RETENTION: spare_consumption records cannot be deleted …"

Reads

  • Every consumption line your role may see — an office role every line, everybody else their own and their team's

Out · 2 of 3 — and in

Stock Transfer — between two engineers

One movement seen from both sides. A transfer is a single record that takes stock out of one balance and puts it into another — so it appears as Transfer out on one ledger and Transfer in on the other, and the two can never disagree, because they are the same row read twice.

Reads

  • Hand Stock of the sender — only parts they hold are offered
  • User Master — names suggested for From and To
  • Part Master — what is moving

Transfer

From one engineer to another

Transfer hand-stock between engineers

＋ New Transfer: From engineer, To engineer, the Date, the parts and quantities, and remarks. The database numbers it ST-2609-0007. The stock moves on the strength of that record alone — there is no dispatch and no acknowledgement — and it is dated by the Date on the form.

The register lists every transfer you may see — searchable, exportable. The From and To boxes suggest User Master names and accept what is typed.

Restrictions & mandates

  • TriggerA transfer cannot overdraw the sender: "Stock transfer exceeds available stock: … would be left with −2 of …". Checked after each line is written, so several lines of the same part on one transfer are judged together.
  • Form"From and To must be different engineers." — and the database refuses the same.
  • PolicyNeeds Transfer hand-stock between engineers; nothing more limits whose stock it moves.

Out · 3 of 3

Material Return — stock goes back to Stores

An MRN sends parts back — and counts good and defective separately, because they are not the same thing arriving back at Stores. Both come off the engineer's hand stock.

Reads

  • Hand Stock of the engineer — only what they hold is offered
  • User Master — names, when returning for someone else

Return

What an MRN records

Return spares to Stores (MRN)

＋ New MRN: the engineer, the MRN No from the paper slip, the MRN Date, each spare with its Good and Defective quantities, and remarks. The database gives the submission its own reference, MRN-2609-0003; the slip number is typed.

The register shows each returned item with its good and defective counts — and Customer, Report No and Removed-from-equipment columns, which the sheet-era returns carry and the New MRN form does not ask for. Open an MRN to see its lines; export to CSV.

Restrictions & mandates

  • TriggerNot more than is held: "Material return exceeds hand stock: … would be left with … of …" — the form caps it first.
  • Form"Enter the MRN number from the slip." · at least one returned per line, good or defective.
  • PolicyYour own stock only, unless you hold Return stock in another engineer’s name.
  • Trigger"A material return cannot be edited — raise a correcting entry instead".

In or out

± Adjust stock — the office's correction

When a balance is wrong, the office corrects the stock — the engineer does not book around it. An adjustment adds to or removes from one engineer's balance of one part, with a reason, and shows on their movements as an Adjustment.

Reads

  • User Master — active names only
  • Part Master — the part
  • Hand Stock — for a removal, what they hold

Adjustment

Add or remove, with a reason

Add consumption against a call (reconciliation)

On Hand Stock, ± Adjust stock: the engineer, the part, Add or Remove, a quantity, the Reason, and a Reference such as the MTN number. It takes effect when saved — there is no second approval. Who recorded it and when are stamped by the database.

It is never edited or deleted. A wrong adjustment is put right by another one the other way, and both stay on the trail with their reasons.

Restrictions & mandates

  • Form"Choose the engineer." · "Choose the part." · "Give a quantity of 1 or more." · "Give the reason — it stays on the record."
  • Trigger"Hand stock is adjusted only for an ACTIVE person on the User Master -- "…" is not one." · "Choose the part from the Part Master -- "…" is not on it."
  • TriggerNever below zero: "… holds … of that part; removing … would take them below zero."
  • PolicyNeeds Add consumption against a call (reconciliation).

Why derived rather than stored

A stored balance is a number somebody can edit, and once it can be edited it has to be reconciled against the movements that were supposed to produce it. Deriving it means the movements are the balance — there is nothing to reconcile, and no way for the two to disagree.

The cost is that every movement has to be right, which is why consumption, transfers, returns and removals are each capped at the balance by the database rather than by a screen.

Report vs Reconciliation

Both are consumption and both move the stock identically. Report is the engineer writing up what they fitted, on a visit; Reconciliation is the office booking a spare that was fitted and never written up — which is why it needs no visit, and does need a reason.

Keeping them tellable apart is what lets somebody ask how much of the register is the engineers' own record and how much was tidied up afterwards.

Void, adjustment, delete

A wrong consumption line is set to 0, not removed: the row keeps its reason and who adjusted it, and the stock returns. A wrong adjustment is answered by another the other way. A material return is corrected by another entry.

Deleting would leave the balance right and the history wrong — and the history is the part an auditor reads. The database refuses the delete of a consumption line outright.
